Overview

Aluminium is light, resists corrosion and machines cleanly. That makes it a strong choice where weight matters or where parts sit outdoors.

The 6000 series alloys are heat treatable and offer a good balance of strength and workability. HE9 and HE30 are British designations commonly matched with 6063 and 6082.

Grade guide

  • 6063 gives a smooth finish and suits architectural and decorative parts
  • 6061 is a versatile structural alloy with good strength and weldability
  • 6082 offers higher strength for load bearing parts and machined components
  • Round, square and hex bars machine easily on lathes and milling machines
  • Sheets and pipes suit enclosures, frames and fluid lines

Bars: Round, square, hex and flat bar up to 400mm

Pipes and sheets: For frames, enclosures and general fabrication
